What's real here

Every position is a real FlyWire neuron; every line is a real synaptic connection. Node brightness comes from a leaky integrate-and-fire simulation using Shiu et al. 2024's published, validated parameters — driving 20 real sugar-sensing neurons and watching activity spread, three to four synapses deep, to the real feeding motor neuron MN9.
